Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a radical new UI CONCEPT for a Windows desktop personal AI companion app named Accompany. The user explicitly requests a bold, paradigm-shifting design, having rejected all conventional left sidebar / center account list / right details layouts. This MUST have a COMPLETELY DIFFERENT INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE and visibly different silhouette. A single crisp, high-fidelity 16:10 landscape product screenshot, no perspective, no device frame, no external poster headline, no watermark.

CONCEPT: "a companion stage with a bottom account rail". A personal conversational instrument, closer to a beautifully crafted creative desktop tool than a system administration dashboard. FULL WIDTH immersive workspace. No left sidebar. No right permanent inspector. No account table or stack of cards in the middle. Navigation is a thin horizontal utility header and a bottom rail of selectable companion accounts. Deep WARM GRAPHITE and matte near-black frame, softly lit warm ivory central canvas, restrained AMBER accent. This should be striking even as a thumbnail because its large composition is fundamentally different.

COMPOSITION:
Top: slim 40px warm charcoal Windows title bar, small "Accompany" at far left, tiny workspace name "私人工作区" beside it. At the horizontal center a simple text navigation "陪伴" active, "工作台", "资源". At far right a tiny icon/status "SnowLuma 已连接", window controls. No rainbow icons, no left navigation.
Main: a warm almost-ivory / mushroom-gray conversational STAGE spanning the full width between the header and bottom rail, with only thin graphite frame edges. Subtly off-white, not plain white. Delicate warm natural light, realistic interface readability. A clearly asymmetric layout:
Left 36% contains a beautifully integrated stylized editorial illustration of the selected fictional companion "青禾": waist-up small dignified young adult woman with botanical olive/amber details, contemporary quiet clothing, softly painted hair and a few autumn leaves. ART is confined to this left part of the stage, not a full wallpaper. Fine painterly linework and understated paper-like shading. Not glossy anime pinup, not photoreal person, no sexualization. It fades subtly into the light stage background at the right. Character stands within an abstract open architectural arch/window shape. Large graceful negative space above and beside her. Near the lower-left, bold but restrained dark typography "青禾", then smaller "绑定 QQ 10001", an understated sage-dot "正在监听", and tiny action links "暂停自动处理" and "账号详情".
Right 60% is a CONVERSATIONAL reading area, spacious and elegant, without comic speech bubbles. A narrow centered column around 600px width. Top small overline "与青禾的会话". A small neutral context chip "当前预览 · 私聊 12345" to indicate whose conversation is being previewed, not an actual all-account blended chat.

At lower part of this transcript column, a minimal standalone composer line with a faint border/underline and placeholder "输入消息，或选择一个动作…", an attachment icon at left and a dark small square send arrow at right, NOT a giant pill. Far upper-right INSIDE stage a single unobtrusive outlined button "实例控制" with a small sliders icon. It opens management controls in the actual product, no permanent inspector in screenshot.

ART DIRECTION:
Sophisticated, bold high contrast BETWEEN a quiet off-white stage and the deep warm graphite rail/frame. Amber only as a selected marker and one main action. This is not a recolor of a standard dark dashboard. It should feel like a private companion atelier with a consistent interaction model. Clean, refined, cinematic through composition rather than glow. No 3D orb, no cyberpunk HUD, no card grid, no background particles, no giant header hero slogan, no random network node diagram. No purple/teal/cyan. No text overlay obscured by illustration. No overloaded labels. Maintain recognizable window controls, keyboard/composer affordances and account identity.
Legible short exact Chinese labels. Static concept only, no arrows pointing at design or external annotations. The radical switch is: FULL WIDTH LIGHT STAGE, large integrated left companion art, right transcript, DARK BOTTOM ACCOUNT RAIL, top horizontal navigation. Absolutely NO vertical sidebar and NO right hand account detail panel.

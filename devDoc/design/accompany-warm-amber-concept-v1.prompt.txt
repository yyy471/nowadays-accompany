Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one new high-fidelity Windows desktop application design screenshot for "Accompany", a personal AI companion app connecting multiple QQ accounts. The user rejected two dark-mint standard dashboard concepts as too templated and now wants to explore WARM DARK GRAY + AMBER. This must be a genuinely different art direction and composition, not a recoloring of a generic admin dashboard.
Landscape 16:10, crisp high-resolution front-on screenshot, the usable desktop application fills the frame. No laptop frame, no perspective, no desk scene, no external poster captions, no watermark.

ART DIRECTION:
An intimate, exquisitely typeset personal desktop workspace with a quiet atelier / reading-room feeling. Warm charcoal #24221F outer background, smoky taupe #302D28 secondary planes, warm ivory #E9E2D5 text, muted warm-gray #B1A89B secondary text, muted amber #DCA960 accent with near-black #211A12 text. Subtle material sophistication, a tiny amount of warm grazing light, extremely subtle fine surface grain, never a leather/wood skeuomorphic texture. Contrast must remain readable. No mint/cyan/teal anywhere. Avoid gaming RGB, orange neon, giant gradient glow, glassmorphism stacks, rounded bento cards, KPI boxes and generic SaaS dashboard styling. ONE principal amber CTA. No sepia filter over a previous UI. The interface should feel deliberately composed and private, with typography, editorial rhythm and purposeful empty space. Professional software, not a website hero or an anime wallpaper.

LAYOUT THAT DEPARTS FROM GENERIC DASHBOARD:
Slim neutral Windows title bar 32px, Accompany wordmark at left, standard minimize/maximize/close at right.
A slim 190px left navigation rail in warm charcoal, with subdued small line icons, grouped navigation labels in legible Chinese: "实例与监听" active, "会话", "活动"; secondary "角色", "插件与 Skill"; lower "模型服务", "资源库", "设置". The active row is a tiny amber square plus ivory text, with NO big filled pill. Small bottom user initials.
The main work surface starts 40px inside the rail and uses a clear two-zone composition:
- Left 65% of remaining space is the operational workspace.
- Right 35% is a quiet ACCOUNT NOTEBOOK region integrated into the same warm surface, separated by one very fine vertical hairline. Not an image drawer and not a large boxed card.
